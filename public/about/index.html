About

Business architecture for the solo operator.

Apex & Axis diagnoses and treats Founder-Dependency Syndrome — the structural condition where revenue, delivery, decision-making, and growth remain locked inside the founder. The firm exists to rebuild the business as an operating system rather than a personal workload.

The methodology is organized across four engines: Marketing, Sales, Operations, and Growth. Each engine removes a different dependency. The objective is not busyness. It is structural decoupling.
