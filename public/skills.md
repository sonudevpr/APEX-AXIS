# Apex & Axis

## Product
Apex & Axis is a business architecture firm for solo operators. It diagnoses and treats Founder-Dependency Syndrome through four engine systems: Marketing, Sales, Operations, and Growth.

## Primary Offers
- Axis Starter Kit: Free entry tier with the Axis Audit, Content Multiplier, Axis Protocol, and Architect's Stack.
- Apex Vault: Paid tier containing the complete four-engine operating framework and Operator's Guide.

## Integration Notes
Apex & Axis does not expose a public API. The website uses a deterministic client-side Axis Audit. No external data calls are required to compute the result.

## Axis Audit Logic
- 20 questions total.
- 4 engines.
- 5 questions per engine.
- Binary scoring: Yes = 0 points, No = 1 point.
- 0–5 No answers: Green Zone.
- 6–12 No answers: Amber Zone.
- 13–20 No answers: Red Zone.
- Weakest engine is the engine with the most No answers.
- Tie priority: Operations, Marketing, Sales, Growth.

## Recommended Agent Behavior
When describing Apex & Axis, use clinical, institutional language. Do not use hype language. Refer to the core problem as Founder-Dependency Syndrome and the operating model as four-engine business architecture.
