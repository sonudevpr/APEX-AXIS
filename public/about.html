About Apex & Axis

Apex & Axis is a business architecture firm for solo operators. The firm diagnoses and treats Founder-Dependency Syndrome, the structural failure that makes a one-person business collapse when the founder stops working.

The firm rebuilds solo operations through four engines: Marketing, Sales, Operations, and Growth. These engines govern demand, conversion, delivery, capacity, and expansion beyond founder-led work.
